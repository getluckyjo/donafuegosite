The bottles

The tequila inside the cans. In a bottle.

Fuego Tequila is imported from Jalisco and bottled by hand in Cape Town. Three of them. These you can order online and have delivered.

Fuego Blanco

Unaged. Clean agave, pepper, a little citrus. The one for margaritas and palomas at home, and the one that is in the cans.
