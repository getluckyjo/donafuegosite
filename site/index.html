Who makes it

A Cape Town distiller who started with tequila.

Danielle Schoeman tasted real tequila in Mexico, came home and taught herself to distil. She founded Doña Distillery in 2019 and has been importing tequila from Jalisco ever since. The cans are that tequila, with real juice, and nothing that needs a footnote.
