Created by

Danielle Schoeman

Danielle tasted proper tequila in Mexico, came home to Cape Town and couldn't find it, so she taught herself to distil. She opened Doña Distillery in 2019. It's female-owned, and tequila is still the reason it exists.

Doña Fuego is that tequila in a can, with real lemon and lime. For this episode she handed out disposable cameras with a list of twelve shots, asked Liezl to bring her Fuji, and let everyone else do the rest.
