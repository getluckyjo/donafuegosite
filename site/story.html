The story

It started with one glass of the real thing.

Danielle Schoeman tasted proper tequila in Mexico, came home to South Africa, and could not find it anywhere. So she learned to make spirits herself.

The distillery

Doña Distillery opened in Cape Town in 2019. It is female-owned and female-run, and in its second year it took national trophies for Best Ready-to-Drink and Best Low and No-Alcohol Drink. Food & Home called Danielle South Africa's youngest female master distiller, which she will tell you is a long title for someone who taught herself in the Karoo.

The distillery makes its own brands and makes drinks for other people too. Tequila was the reason it exists, and it is still the thing Danielle cares about most.

The tequila

Nobody distils tequila in South Africa, because by law tequila is made in Mexico from blue agave. Doña Distillery has imported it from Jalisco for years, in bulk, and bottles it in Cape Town as Fuego Tequila. That is the tequila in every can.

The Spicy Margarita gets its heat from jalapeño steeped in that tequila at the distillery, not from a flavouring added at the end. The juice is real lemon and lime, and real grapefruit in the Paloma.

Why a can

A margarita at a braai is a good idea that takes a bottle, a mixer, limes, salt, ice and a shaker to pull off. Most Saturdays nobody does. The can is the same drink with the admin taken out, so it goes in the cooler box next to everything else and comes out cold.

Four of them: Margarita, Spicy Margarita, Paloma, and a Non-Alc Margarita for whoever is not drinking today. In the fridge at Checkers from August 2026.
