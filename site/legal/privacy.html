Privacy

What we collect, why, and how to make us delete it. Last updated 21 October 2026.

Draft for attorney review. Written to the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) for the site build. Not yet checked by a lawyer. Replace this notice once it has.

Who is responsible

Doña Distillery (Pty) Ltd, Cape Town, South Africa, is the responsible party. Information officer: name TBC, reachable through the contact page.

What we collect, and why

  • Email sign-up. Your email address, and your name if you give it. We use it to send you the emails you asked for: new cans, where we are pouring, the occasional giveaway. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw by unsubscribing.
  • Contact form. Your name, email address and message. We use it to reply. Lawful basis: it is necessary to answer you.
  • Age confirmation. Your answer to the age gate is stored in your own browser only. It never reaches us.
  • Site usage. We count visits with Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics: which pages are read, roughly where from (country and city), on what kind of device, and which links to Checkers get tapped. Google Analytics runs in its no-cookie mode, so it cannot recognise you from one visit to the next, and Vercel's counter sets no cookies either. Neither one gets your name or email address. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site works.

We do not collect anything else, and we do not buy, sell or swap lists.

Who else sees it

Email addresses and messages are stored and sent with Resend, an email service provider. The site is hosted by Vercel, and visit counts go to Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics. All three may process data outside South Africa under their own safeguards. Nobody else gets your details unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Sign-ups: until you unsubscribe, after which the address is removed from the list. Contact messages: for as long as it takes to deal with them and up to two years after, in case you write again.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us through the contact page and we will do it within a reasonable time, normally a week. You can also complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Under 18s

This site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

Cookies

The site sets no tracking cookies. Google Analytics is set to run without them. The age gate uses local storage in your browser so you are not asked every time, and the title sequence remembers for one visit that it has played.

Changes

If this changes we will update the date at the top and, for anything material, tell subscribers by email.
