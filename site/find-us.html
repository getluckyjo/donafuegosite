Where to get it

At Checkers LiquorShop.

In shops, the cans are only at Checkers. Singles, four-packs and cases of 24 at LiquorShop stores nationally, and on Sixty60 when the cooler box is already packed.

Checkers LiquorShopIn store

Look with the other spirit coolers, in the fridge or on the shelf. If your store does not have it yet, ask the manager. They can order it in.

Bars and restaurants

Want it on your list?

Checkers is the only shop that sells the cans, but bars and restaurants can put them on the menu. The Fuego Tequila bottles are at Checkers, Pick n Pay and Spar, and open to the whole trade. If you run a bar or a restaurant and want either, or a bottle store that wants the bottles, talk to us.
