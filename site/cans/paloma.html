Paloma

Made with real tequila, grapefruit, lemon & lime juice

In Mexico the paloma is what people actually drink. Grapefruit first, tequila under it, lime at the end to sharpen it up. Lighter than a margarita, a bit bitter, very easy to have two of.

  • Size250ml
  • Alcohol7% alc. vol.
  • TequilaReal, imported from Jalisco
  • JuiceGrapefruit, lemon and lime
  • ClassSpirit cooler
  • PackFour-pack at Checkers

How to drink it

Over ice with a wedge of grapefruit squeezed in. A pinch of salt on top if the afternoon is hot.
