Spicy Margarita

Made with real jalapeño-infused tequila, lemon & lime juice

The jalapeño goes into the tequila, not into the can afterwards. So the heat is in the spirit itself and builds through the drink instead of sitting on top of it. Green, warm, a bit smoky at the end.

How to drink it

Over ice with a chilli-salt rim if you have one, plain salt if you do not. Keeps up with anything that came off the grid.

Where it goes

Next to the fire. With the boerewors rolls. For the one who always asks for the hot sauce.
