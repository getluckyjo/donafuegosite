Margarita

Made with real tequila, lemon & lime juice

The one on the label. Tequila from Jalisco, lemon, lime, a little sweetness, and that is the end of the list. It tastes like the margarita you would make at home if you could be bothered, which on a Saturday you cannot.

How to drink it

Cold from the can is the point. If there is a glass and ice nearby, pour it over, run a lime wedge round the rim and dip it in salt.

Where it goes

The braai. The beach. The one you hand to someone who says they do not like tequila.
